Motion Party

Games you play with your whole body. Your phone watches every step, jump and squat.

Before you move

  • Clear about 2 meters of space around you.
  • Hold the phone firmly. Two hands is best.
  • Stay away from stairs, glass tables, pets and small kids.
  • Jumping is optional. Skip it if it's not safe for you.

Demo mode (no motion sensors). Keys: W step · J jump · S squat · U up · T turn · K shake · F flip · mouse = hand shake
